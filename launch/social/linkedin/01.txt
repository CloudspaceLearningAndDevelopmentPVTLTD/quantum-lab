A probability distribution is never negative. This one is.

That is the Wigner function of a Schrödinger-cat state — one superposition of two coherent states — turning through a single period of its own harmonic evolution. Gold is positive. Ice is below zero. The minimum sits at −0.238, and 18.9% of the landscape's total weight is under the floor.

The strip along the bottom is the part a detector can actually see: the state projected onto one quadrature. Turn the detector and the same state reads as two separate lumps at 0° and as an interference comb at 90°. Both of those are ordinary, non-negative probability distributions. The landscape they are shadows of is not — and that is the piece no classical model reproduces.
